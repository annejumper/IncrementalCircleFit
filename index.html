Incremental circle-fit modal analysis

Open an ObieApp test folder: the one containing TRF/ and template.json. You can also pick a folder of several tests and choose one.

Click a peak to circle-fit it, accept it to subtract it from every FRF, and repeat. The analytical plate panel compares your modes with thin-plate theory.

Everything runs in your browser. Your data files are read locally and never uploaded.

Analytical plate

Thin cantilever plate, clamped along one edge (the clamp can be an elastic, rotational spring). Tick vary for the settings Optimize may change.

Peaks are your accepted modes, paired one-to-one with analytical modes by frequency (not by mode shape). Thickness, E and density only matter together as h·√(E/ρ), so vary at most one of them.

Options

seconds of zeros after each recording (0 = off)

Zero padding: rebuilds the FRFs from the raw hit recordings (raw/*.wav, after your time cutoff) with this many seconds of zeros added to each. The bins get finer, e.g. 2 s + 6 s of zeros gives 4× more points per peak. It interpolates what was recorded rather than adding information; only longer recordings truly increase resolution.
